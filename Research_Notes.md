# Research Notes

1. Abstract

Cryptocurrency has emerged as an important digital financial asset class characterized by high price volatility, rapidly changing market capitalization, and substantial fluctuations in trading volume. This research focuses on analyzing cryptocurrency market capitalization, trading volume, price movements, and volatility trends using historical market data.

The proposed study uses Python for data cleaning and Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), SQL for data querying and aggregation, and Power BI for interactive visualization. Important indicators such as total market capitalization, total trading volume, average cryptocurrency price, volatility, and cryptocurrency-wise performance are analyzed.

Previous research has examined relationships between cryptocurrency price, trading volume, volatility, liquidity, and market capitalization using statistical and econometric techniques. For example, Adediran et al. studied price-volume relationships using CoinMarketCap data and applied correlation, VAR, and Granger causality methods.  Recent research has also examined the relationship between volatility, traded volume, and price across crypto-assets. 

The proposed project combines these variables into an integrated data-analysis framework suitable for understanding cryptocurrency market trends through visual analytics.

2. Introduction

Cryptocurrency is a digital asset that uses cryptographic techniques and blockchain technology to enable decentralized transactions. Bitcoin, Ethereum and thousands of other cryptocurrencies are traded globally through cryptocurrency exchanges.

The cryptocurrency market can be studied using several important indicators:

Market Capitalization

Trading Volume

Price

Returns

Volatility

Liquidity

Market Share


Market capitalization is commonly used to represent the relative size of a cryptocurrency, while trading volume provides information about market activity.

The relationship between price and trading volume has been investigated in academic research. Adediran, Babajide and Osina analyzed Bitcoin, Ethereum and Tether using CoinMarketCap daily data from 2016–2021 and found different price-volume relationships across the cryptocurrencies. 

Another study analyzed volatility, traded volume and price across a large set of crypto-assets and reported a positive relationship between traded volume and volatility. 

Therefore, analyzing these variables together can provide a broader view of cryptocurrency market behavior.

3. Problem Statement

Cryptocurrency market data contains large amounts of information that change continuously.

Investors, researchers and analysts need to understand:

1. How cryptocurrency market capitalization changes over time.


2. How trading volume changes over time.


3. Which cryptocurrencies have the highest market capitalization.


4. Which cryptocurrencies have the highest trading volume.


5. Whether higher trading activity is associated with higher volatility.


6. How different cryptocurrencies behave during different periods.


7. How market trends can be presented using interactive dashboards.



The project addresses these requirements using a combination of Python, SQL and Power BI.
